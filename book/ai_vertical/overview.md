# AI Vertical Overview

This part explores how AI becomes useful in specific industries and business workflows. It connects the model methods introduced earlier in the book to the people, systems, and outcomes that define an application.

We use **AI Vertical** broadly to include both industry-specific applications and specialized workflows that span industries. Contact centers, for example, serve retail, travel, financial services, and many other sectors.

## How to read these case studies

For each application, ask:

- **Value:** What customer or business outcome should improve?
- **Workflow:** What can the system do, and where does a person remain involved?
- **Information and actions:** What context and external systems does the application need?
- **Model methods:** Which gaps call for prompting, retrieval, or training?
- **Evaluation:** What evidence would demonstrate success in real use?

Speaker insights are attributed to their sources. Additional examples and design questions are identified as this book's analysis.
